Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. This policy explains how Visa Bridge LTD uses them on this website.

What we use

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We use only what is needed to deliver the site, which does not require your consent under UK rules.

Third-party services

  • Hosting (Vercel): processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site.
  • Request form (Formspree): receives what you submit, and may use technical measures to stop spam.
  • Stripe and WhatsApp: if you follow a payment link or the WhatsApp button, those services have their own cookies and privacy policies.

If this changes

If we add analytics or marketing tools in future, we will ask for your consent through a cookie banner before they run, and update this page.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some may affect how third-party sites work. For questions, email info@visabridge.online. More about how we use data is in our Privacy Policy.
