Frequently asked questions

How quickly will you respond?

We aim to respond to every request within 24 hours.

Do I need to know which visa I need?

No. Tell us what you want to do and where you want to go. We will review your situation and explain the available options.

How much does the service cost?

After reviewing your request, we provide a clear offer with the applicable service fee and explain any government or third-party costs separately where applicable.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant government or immigration authority. VisaBridge provides assistance and handles the agreed process but cannot guarantee a government decision.

How do I pay?

Once you accept our offer, payment can be made securely through a Stripe payment link.

Can you help with residency?

Yes, where the relevant residency route is available for your nationality and circumstances.

Can you help if I don't know which visa I need?

Yes. This is exactly what our initial assessment is designed to help with.

Do you provide relocation support?

Yes. Depending on the destination, we can assist with immigration, documentation and practical relocation requirements through our local partner network.

Can you convert my driver's licence?

We can assist with driver's licence and document conversion procedures where such services are available.

How long does the process take?

Timeframes depend on the destination, visa type, nationality, documents and government processing times. Your offer will explain the expected timeframe.
